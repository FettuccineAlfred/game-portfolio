Ring Around the Rabbit (2025)

Ring Around the Rabbit (abbreviated as RAtR, despite me continuing to misspell it as ROtR) holds a very special place in my heart. Prior to it, I had mentioned to my friends multiple times about wanting to make a video game. It kinda became my thing. And, sure, I made The Rhythm Gehenna RPG and Dazzle Disaster, but that was with a different group of friends, and was also made based on RPG Maker MV. So when my good friend Claire suggested we participate, and my friends were down to do so, I was stoked. It was a pretty laborious weekend, but we ended up with a wholly original project that scored really well for a first try! I think this game is what solidified my want to make video games for a living. If we can make something like this, what else could we do?
